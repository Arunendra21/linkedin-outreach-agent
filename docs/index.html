The pipeline

Five steps, connected end to end

Each stage feeds the next. The dashboard lights up a step as the one before it finishes, and a live console streams every action.

1

Your profile

Who the messages come from, plus your send limits and confidence threshold.

2

Connect LinkedIn

A one-time login in a dedicated browser profile. Reused on every run after.

3

Find people

Searches for software engineers and recent grads at the company you name.

4

Verify emails

Resolves the real mail domain and scores each address, so you don't bounce.

5

Review & send

Pick who to reach. Connections and interview-prep emails go out, human-paced.

Why it's different

Built to help you, not to get you flagged

✦

Verified, not guessed

Hunter.io confirms the domain and address. Anything under your confidence bar is skipped, so your sender reputation stays clean.

◷

Human pace, hard caps

Randomized delays and daily ceilings on both connections and emails keep activity well inside sane limits.

⊘

Never double-contacts

Everyone you reach is logged locally. Re-run all you like — nobody hears from you twice.

✎

Personalized, in your voice

Notes and emails are written per person and stay under LinkedIn's limits. No em dashes, no templated tells.

⇄

Two channels

A LinkedIn connection with a note and a direct email — or either one alone, your call per run.

⌂

Local & private

Runs on your machine against your own session and inbox. Credentials live in files only you can read.

⚖️

Use it like a person would

Automated connecting sits against LinkedIn's terms, and cold email is a manners game. This tool is assistive: small batches, honest messages, your accounts, your responsibility. Keep the volume low and the intent genuine and it works the way real networking does.

Get started

Clone it and run in two minutes

Ships as full source with a web dashboard and a CLI. Open source, self-hosted, yours to change.

setup once
# grab it and install
git clone <your-repo-url> outreach-agent
cd outreach-agent
python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
cp config.example.yaml config.yaml   # edit me
launch the dashboard web
./run_web.sh
# then open http://localhost:8000

# prefer the terminal? one command:
python -m outreach.main "Stripe" --limit 8
